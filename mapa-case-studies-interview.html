Three Lessons, One Approach

1. Communication is the foundation
Right questions, right time, right person
2. Quality is non-negotiable
Essential controls are maintained even under pressure
3. Prioritization is about trade-offs
Decisions need clear criteria and triggers

Why this approach works for MAPA: Aligned with the company's core values — Accountability, Responsiveness, Precision, Proactivity, Consistency — and demonstrates practical project management judgment.
